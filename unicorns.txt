Unicorns

Unicorns are legendary creatures from mythology and folklore, typically depicted as horse-like animals with a single spiraling horn projecting from their forehead. They have appeared in the myths of ancient Greece, the Middle Ages, and across many world cultures.

In European tradition, the unicorn is a symbol of purity, grace, and untamed freedom. Medieval lore held that only a person of pure heart could approach or tame a unicorn, and that its horn — called an alicorn — had the magical power to neutralize poison and purify water.

Unicorns are commonly portrayed as white, though depictions vary across cultures. In heraldry, the unicorn is a prominent symbol, most notably in the royal coat of arms of Scotland and the United Kingdom, where it represents power and independence.

In modern popular culture, unicorns have become icons of fantasy and whimsy, appearing in literature, film, and art as symbols of magic and wonder. From the majestic creatures of classic mythology to the rainbow-maned figures of contemporary imagination, unicorns continue to capture the human love of mystery and the extraordinary.
